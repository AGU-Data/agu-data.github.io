# Open Science Vision 
## Evolving Data / Open Science Leadership at AGU in its second decade. 

## The AGU Data / Open Science Leadership Program is moving into its second decade. Over this time we have expanded from a data focus to include other research digital objects in context of the various types of openness in the research ecosystem. 

***How does this vision compare to your own work in Open Science?***


### AGU Open Science Vision
> * Data, software, and sample management practices are integrated in the research lifecycle for all Earth, space and environmental scientists globally
> * Researchers are recognized and rewarded for all aspects of collaboration
> * Researchers are able to make open access the norm for sharing their research, regardless of their funding
> * Researchers have discipline-specific, well-established, managed, and published best practices that are endorsed and adopted by their global community
> * Research samples, instruments, software, and tools are aligned on data and software best practices
> * Research ecosystem fully enables FAIR and CARE for data, software, and samples
> * Research ecosystem is fully democratized and inclusive of all aspects in all places from all voices
> * Research communities have the means to monitor and track progress on open science practices
